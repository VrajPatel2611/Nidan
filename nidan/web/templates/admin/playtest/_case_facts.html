{# The case's ground truth, for a clinician who has been asked to score it. Shown ONLY when `review` is set — that is, when `?review=1` brought a reviewer here from their queue. Playtest without it stays what it was: a case played blind, which is the right way to feel whether a trap works. It exists because the rubric asks four questions that cannot be answered from the student view. "Internal consistency" means do the investigation results agree with the diagnosis; "solvability from the information given" means is the answer reachable from what is actually in the case; and neither is visible to someone who can only ask the patient questions. A reviewer without this panel is scoring from whatever they happened to elicit, and two reviewers would be scoring different things. **This is not a CT-1 concern.** CT-1 guards `api/v1/case_view.py`, the one place a case becomes a *learner's* response, and nothing here touches it. This is `/admin`, behind `@require_reviewer`, reading `CaseRepository.for_review()` — the method that exists precisely so a reviewer may read a draft in full. The eight fields `PRD` FR-3.4 forbids in a learner payload are exactly the eight a reviewer is here to check. #}
